About PSIT

Pranveer Singh Institute of Technology (PSIT), established in 2004, is one of the leading engineering institutes in North India. It is approved by AICTE, recognized under UGC 2(f), and affiliated with Dr. A.P.J. Abdul Kalam Technical University (AKTU), Lucknow.
